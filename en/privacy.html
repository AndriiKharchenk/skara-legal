Privacy Policy

Skara · Updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what data the Skara app uses and how it is handled. Rights holder: Skara.

1. Camera and images

The app needs the camera: it uses it to determine head position, eyes and mouth. The image is processed only on your phone at the moment of capture. Video and photos are not recorded, not saved and not shared with third parties.

2. Data stored on the device

  • Trip history: start and end time, number of alert activations. Stored only on the phone, for no more than 30 days.
  • Technical operation log: numeric readings (for example, head angle, eye openness) without images. Size is limited to 20 MB, files older than 7 days are deleted automatically.
  • Settings (for example, vibration).
  • Access information: the date of first launch, and whether a purchase was made or a promo code was activated. The promo code is checked on the phone itself and is not sent anywhere.
  • Android backup: trip history, settings and access information may be saved automatically in your Google account backup (Google Drive) and restored after reinstalling or on a new phone. We have no access to this backup; you can turn it off in Android settings. The technical log is not included.

3. Google account

If you sign in with a Google account, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo — only to display them in the app. This data is stored on your phone and is not sent to our servers; to load the photo the app contacts Google's servers. When you sign out in the settings, the profile data is deleted from the phone.

4. Feedback

A message to support is sent only at your wish, together with the text you wrote yourself.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell data, do not show ads and do not pass data to advertising services. Data does not leave the phone, except in cases you initiate yourself. Purchases (when enabled) are processed by Google Play: we do not receive or store payment details.

6. Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not designed for people under 18.

7. Deleting data

All app data is deleted when the app is removed from the phone (except the Android backup, which you can delete in Google settings). You can also clear it without uninstalling — in Android settings, "Apps → Skara → Storage → Clear data". For questions about your data, write to: skarasupport@gmail.com.

8. Changes

The policy may be updated. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the screen; we will notify you of significant changes in the app.
